    About Us

    Story Field Guide Projects turns subjects into places you can wander. Each field guide takes a body of information (a century of songs, an island prison, a galaxy of starships, a shelf of banned books) and gives it a shape: a map, a timeline, a trail, a mix tape.

    We build them by utilizing AI tools to help data take form and allow users to engage in the experience. Sources are gathered and organized, then turned into something you can move through, zoom into and play with, rather than simply read.

    Every guide is made with AI assistance from Anthropic and OpenAI and is meant to be explored at your own pace: walk the trail, open a guide, follow your curiosity.
