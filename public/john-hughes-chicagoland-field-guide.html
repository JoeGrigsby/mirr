Field Guide № 009

Sources & Method

This guide maps the John Hughes films set in, filmed in, or strongly associated with Chicago and its suburbs. Its organizing tension is real place versus fictional place: Shermer, Illinois does not exist, but the houses, schools and streets that made it do.

Three layers

  • As seen on screen — where the story says a scene happens. Shermer locations gather into one fictional town. Chicago footage presented as New York, St. Louis or an unnamed roadside moves to a postcard for that story place.
  • Where it was shot — the filming location as listed in the source. Exteriors shot outside Illinois (the Bueller house, Long Beach, California) are annotated, not plotted.
  • What’s there now — present-day status. Demolished, changed and unconfirmed sites are marked distinctly.

Labels

  • Source — stated in the compiled location list.
  • Editorial · verify — added by the guide editors from general knowledge; unconfirmed until checked.
  • Disputed — the source hedges, or the placement conflicts with other information.
  • Doubling — a Chicago location standing in for another city.
  • Verified: not yet — no entry has yet been checked against a primary record.

Research still to do

  • Production records and call sheets; municipal and Chicago Film Office location permits; contemporaneous local press.
  • Then-and-now photographs with rights cleared, credited per image.
  • Site visits to confirm present-day status, from the public way.

The map

The lakeshore, rivers, interstates, Lake Shore Drive, Metra Union Pacific North and Northwest lines, and Shermer Road are traced from OpenStreetMap (© OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL), retrieved October 2026, and drawn to scale. Town dots sit at their real coordinates. The thin white lines follow Chicago’s one-mile arterial grid. Shaded green areas mark real forest preserves and parks, drawn as simplified shapes. Trees, boats, clouds and figures are decoration. Pins spread apart slightly so they don’t overlap, and towns beyond the core area are drawn as “off scale” tags at the edges. Private homes are placed at town level regardless of the street address in the source.
